Start here

The Studio shows one piece of music in many ways. Each view answers a different question. This guide says which question, how to read every line, dot and colour, and where a view can mislead you.

If you have twenty minutes, take the first tour: one real piece, eight views, in order. Then the second: a generated piece, the same questions. If you only want to know what some line means, go to Every line, dot and colour.

The three pieces used here

  • September: Tchaikovsky, The Seasons Op. 37a No. 9, a real performance transcribed to MIDI (3:07). A clear A-B-A′, with expert labels for its keys, chords and cadences.
  • Generated piece 16 (2-p2_16, 2:15): the model's own. It states one 35-second section three times: A A B A.
  • Generated piece 12 (2-p2_12, 3:00): no section returns, but one short idea does.

The zip copy of this guide also holds a projects folder with these three projects. In the Studio use Project, Open project... and choose the project.json inside one of them, or copy the folders beside your other projects.

Every picture was drawn by the Studio's own code at the Large text size. Click a picture to see it at full size; the numbers on it can be hidden.

Evidence, not verdicts. The views show evidence. None of them judges a piece, and none of them knows what you like. A clear form and a piece you love are two different things.
