Read more

If you read one thing, read chapter 4 of the first entry, or its free notebooks.

Start

  • M. Müller, Fundamentals of Music Processing, 2nd ed., Springer 2021 (the book behind most of these views)
  • The FMP notebooks: the book's free companion, with code, pictures and sound; chapter 4 (music structure) is the one this program follows, chapter 6 is rhythm and tempo, chapter 3 chroma
  • Müller and Zalkow, libfmp, JOSS 2021, and its code: the reference the Studio's SSM, thumbnail and novelty follow

Structure

  • J. Foote, Visualizing music and audio using self-similarity, ACM Multimedia 1999: where the self-similarity matrix began
  • J. Foote, Automatic audio segmentation using a measure of audio novelty, ICME 2000: the checkerboard kernel
  • Müller, Jiang and Grosche, A robust fitness measure for capturing repetitions in music recordings, IEEE TASLP 2013
  • Müller and Jiang, A scape plot representation for visualizing repetitive structures of music recordings, ISMIR 2012 (PDF)
  • Paulus, Müller and Klapuri, Audio-based music structure analysis, ISMIR 2010 (PDF): repetition, novelty and homogeneity side by side
  • Nieto, Mysore, Wang and Smith, Audio-based music structure analysis: current trends, open challenges, and applications, TISMIR 2020

Sound, rhythm, timbre

  • J. Brown, Calculation of a constant Q spectral transform, JASA 1991
  • Grosche, Müller and Kurth, Cyclic tempogram, ICASSP 2010
  • Davis and Mermelstein, 1980 (where MFCCs come from) and B. Logan, Mel frequency cepstral coefficients for music modeling, ISMIR 2000 (PDF)

Musical form and harmony

  • Open Music Theory: a free textbook; its chapters on harmony and form cover keys, Roman numerals and cadences
  • W. Caplin, Classical Form, Oxford 1998 (the chapter on basic formal functions)
  • The DCML annotation standard: how the keys, chords, phrases and cadences are written
  • DCML's annotated edition of Tchaikovsky's The Seasons, the source of the September labels (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)

Expectation

  • D. Huron, Sweet Anticipation, MIT Press 2006, and L. Meyer, Emotion and Meaning in Music, 1956: expectation and surprise
