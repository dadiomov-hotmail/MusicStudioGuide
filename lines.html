Every line, dot and colour

The quick answer to "what is that line?" The same colour can mean different things in different views, so look under the view you are in.

Everywhere

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Red vertical linePlayback cursor.
Pale vertical line that follows the mouseHover position (raw views and stack).
Black to white through purple, red, yellowLeast to most. The legend line gives the numbers.

Self-similarity matrix (M)

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Blue vertical band, two solid blue edgesSelected segment (X).
Green horizontal band, green bar at the leftAn occurrence of the selected segment (Y).
Solid cyan lineHow an occurrence lines up with the segment.
Bright stripeA repeated passage.

Scape plot (T)

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Dashed white ringBest thumbnail.
Solid blue ringSelected segment.
Solid green ringAn occurrence.
Blue and green bars belowThe same on the time axis.

Novelty (N)

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Solid blue horizontal boxSelected scale.
Solid blue curveNovelty at that scale; its top is its own maximum.
Yellow dotPeak of the selected scale.
White dotsPeaks of other scales.
Faint vertical lineA selected peak carried through all scales.
Yellow tick at the bottomPersistence.
Black steps at the sidesExcluded edges.

Aspect stack (K)

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Curve in the aspect's colourNovelty of that aspect, scaled to its own maximum.
DotPeak.
Yellow tickPersistence.
Grey endsExcluded edges.

Raw aspect (R)

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Thin grey line, or grey dotsRaw values.
Thick coloured lineSmoothed values, the ones that count.
Thin white horizontal linesAxis levels: note names, semitones, velocity, dB, BPM.
Solid cyan line (tempo)Tempo estimate, 50 to 200 BPM.
Dashed lime line (tempo)Strongest tempo, 30 to 480 BPM.
Blue, white, red (timbre)Below, at, above average.

Spectrogram (S) and chromagram (C)

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Thin horizontal linesFrequency marks; borders between pitch classes.

Plan lane (P)

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Green bracketPhrase.
Blue curveIntensity arc.
Red dotted lineEvent.
HatchingTiming not trustworthy.
Green tintTwo plans agree.
Grey dotA chord with no room for its name.

Piano roll and lanes

Looks likeWhat it isHow to read and use it
Yellow bandSustain pedal.
Blue strip at the bottomSoft pedal.
Black outlineTop-line note.
Blue curve in a laneA measured series.
